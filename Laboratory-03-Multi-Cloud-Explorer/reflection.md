# Mission 3 Reflection — Outline Guide

> This checkpoint asks for a genuine 250–350 word personal reflection.
> Below is a paragraph-by-paragraph outline with guiding prompts —
> fill in your own real thoughts/experience, not generic answers,
> since this is exactly the kind of "own analysis" the syllabus's
> Academic Integrity Policy asks you to contribute yourself.

## Paragraph 1 — Which platform impressed you most? (60–80 words)
- Name the platform and ONE specific thing you saw while researching
  (e.g., a specific feature, the console's UI, a case study) that stood out.
- Say *why* it stood out to you specifically.

## Paragraph 2 — Similarities among AWS, Azure, and GCP 
- Think about: pay-as-you-go pricing, regions/availability zones,
  IAM-style identity systems, managed Kubernetes offerings, web-based
  consoles + CLI tools.
- Pick 2–3 you found most interesting or unexpected.

## Paragraph 3 — How business requirements drive platform choice 
- Refer back to the four client scenarios (Checkpoint 4) — what did
  comparing them teach you about "no single best cloud provider"?
- Mention one specific requirement (budget, existing tech stack,
  AI workload, global scale) that changed your recommendation.

## Paragraph 4 — What you learned as a "Cloud Solutions Consultant" 
- Was it more about technical facts, or about weighing trade-offs
  and communicating a recommendation?
- Any moment where your first instinct (e.g., "just pick AWS, it's biggest")
  was wrong once you dug into a scenario?

## Paragraph 5 — How your GitHub Portfolio has improved 
- Concretely: how many labs/repos so far, what skills does this add
  (Markdown documentation, comparison research, multi-cloud thinking)?
- How does this build toward a portfolio you'd show an employer?
